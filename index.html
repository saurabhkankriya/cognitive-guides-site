What we're building toward

Thinking tools should be useful, not just interesting.

Most of us have heard of mental models. Fewer of us remember to use them at the right moment. Cognitive Guides aims to close that gap between knowing an idea and applying it.

◇

Understand the idea

Clear explanations of cognitive biases, laws, heuristics, and mental models without unnecessary jargon.

◎

Recognize the moment

Relatable situations and examples that help you recognize when a thinking tool might be useful.

↗

Apply it thoughtfully

Practical prompts to challenge assumptions, weigh trade-offs, and make more considered choices.
